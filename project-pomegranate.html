Pomegranate Playing Card – Illustration Concept

An illustrative concept inspired by the rich colors, distinctive structure, and symbolic beauty of the pomegranate. The design reimagines a traditional playing card by incorporating pomegranate-inspired shapes and details, blending fruit elements with classic card aesthetics. The composition explores symmetry, decorative patterns, and vibrant colors to create a unique visual interpretation that combines traditional playing card design with playful illustration.
